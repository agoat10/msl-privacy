Metro Soccer League App: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This app is the official app of the Metro Soccer League in Calgary, Alberta. This policy explains what information the app shows and how it is handled.

What the app does not collect

The app does not ask users to create an account, sign in, or enter any personal information. It does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics, and it does not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers.

What the app shows

The app displays league information supplied by the league: team names and logos, player names, shirt numbers, player photos, match results, statistics, news posts, and announcements. Player information is shown with the permission of the league and its players.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores the league data shown in the app and provides the sign-in used by league administrators only. Firebase may process technical information such as IP addresses when the app loads data.
  • Cloudinary hosts the photos shown in the app.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone else.

Children

Some players may be under 18. Photos and names of minors are only shown with parent or guardian permission obtained by the league.

Removal requests

If you or your child appears in the app and you want your name, number, or photo changed or removed, email us and we will update it promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
